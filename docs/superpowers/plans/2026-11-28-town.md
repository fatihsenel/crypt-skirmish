# The Village Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A Town panel at camp after each act's boss where the party spends gold rebuilding the smith, the temple and the library, each stage making the rest of the run easier outside fights.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in a new `src/game/town.js` (like `shop.js`); the pedlar's stock and prices read the stages through `shop.js`; the after-room and act-break effects go in `completeFight`. A `ui/town.js` panel like `ui/shop.js`, a Town button beside the pedlar's, and in Play together a voted `town` step in `CoopRun`.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-town-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Stages cost **120, 220 and 340 gold**; three buildings: `smith`, `temple`, `library`, stages 0–3.
- Nothing reaches a fight's rules or dice: no `RULES` bump, fight records and links unchanged.
- No town in the Endless Crypt (`state.endless`), dailies, weeklies or links; New Game+ starts in ruins.
- No new `track()` event or property.
- `src/game/` stays pure (no DOM, no three.js, no `Math.random`); `ai.js` unchanged.
- A save without `town` loads as all zeros (`cleanTown` in `parseSave`).

## Review Focus

- A save from before the town (no `town` field) at an act break: Town opens with all stages 0, nothing throws.
- Buying a stage twice from two co-op clicks: the second `town` step no longer fits and is skipped (gold can't go negative).
- Temple 3 on a hero with no spell slots (fighter) or full slots: nothing changes; a warlock's slots are already full.
- Library 3 with no hero who can read any scroll: no scroll handed out, no crash.
- The final room (`done`) and Endless floors: no Town button, `buildProblem` says why.

### Task 2: What the stages do

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/game/shop.js` (`stock`, `priceOf`, `sellPrice(id, state)`), `src/game/campaign.js` (`completeFight`), `src/ui/shop.js`, `src/game/coopRun.js` (sell check)
- Test: `tests/town.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `priceOf(state, id) -> number` (library 2: scrolls `Math.round(PRICES[id] * 2 / 3)`), `sellPrice(id, state)` (smith 2: `Math.floor(PRICES[id] * 3 / 4)`), `stock` (smith 1: three gear; smith 3: pool as if one more act cleared; library 1: two scrolls, distinct). `completeFight`'s report gains `town: { hp, slots, potions, scrolls: [[code, id]] } | null`.
- Temple 1: +3 HP each hero after every room that isn't an act break or the last, capped at max (alongside Hesk). Temple 3: each hero with `slotMax[1] > 0` and `slots[1] < slotMax[1]` gets one level-1 slot back, same rooms. Temple 2: a potion each at an act break. Library 3: at an act break, each hero who can read a scroll gets one, `SCROLLS` picked by a steady hash of the room and hero among those they read. None of it in the Endless Crypt.

- [ ] Tests: each stage's effect, and stage 0 unchanged from today; then implement, then PASS; `tests/shop.test.js` still passes.
