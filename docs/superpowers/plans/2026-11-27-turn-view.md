# Turning the View Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the battle view in quarter turns (Q/E, a Turn button, a two-finger twist, LT/RT), with wall fading and the brazier shafts following the view.

**Architecture:** The camera orbits `controls.target` by ±π/2 of azimuth (`stage.turn`). The view direction is read back from the camera as a diagonal `{ x: ±1, z: ±1 }` (`stage.view()`), and pure helpers in `src/render/view.js` turn it into wall fading, compass words and twist turns. Nothing in `src/game/` changes.

**Tech Stack:** three r187 (OrthographicCamera, MapControls), Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-turn-view-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rules change: `src/game/` untouched, no `RULES` bump.
- Under `calm()` the turn snaps; otherwise it eases over 300 ms and calls `keepAwake`.
- A new fight starts from the usual corner (camera at +x, +z of the target).
- Photo mode restores the view it was entered from (it already saves position and target).
- Turns are announced through `announce()`: "View from the south-east" (north is −z, east is +x).
- Commit only when the user asks: the tasks below end without commits.

## Review Focus

1. Turning while a camera move runs (stair tilt, last blow, boss sweep): the newer move wins and the camera ends level, never between quarters — `turn` takes a `panTween` ticket like other pans.
2. Turning in photo mode or during a replay's end banner: Q/E do nothing in photo mode (`photo().active`); in a replay they turn.
3. A twist that also pinches: zoom keeps working; the turn fires once per twist, not every frame.
4. Phone row width at 375 px with the Turn button and Floor shown: `npm run touch-check` must still say the layout fits.
5. Wall fading from each of the four sides fades the wall between camera and figure, not the one behind — pinned by the view tests.

### Task 1: View helpers

**Files:**
- Create: `src/render/view.js`
- Test: `tests/view.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `viewOf(dx: number, dz: number) -> { x: 1|-1, z: 1|-1 }` — the camera's ground offset from its target, as signs.
  - `inFront(w: {x,z}, p: {x,z}, view) -> boolean` — the wall `w` hides figure `p` from a camera on `view`: depth `(w−p)·view` in (0.5, 3.5) and side `|(w−p)×view|` < 1.6 (the numbers `tickFade` uses today).
  - `compass(view) -> string` — `{1,1}` 'south-east', `{-1,1}` 'south-west', `{-1,-1}` 'north-west', `{1,-1}` 'north-east'.
  - `twistTurn(radians: number) -> -1|0|1` — 0 below 35° (0.61 rad); sign of the angle otherwise.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `tests/view.test.js`:
  - `viewOf(30, 30)` is `{x:1,z:1}`; `viewOf(-30, 30)` is `{x:-1,z:1}`.
  - from `{1,1}`, a wall at (5,5) is in front of a figure at (4,4) and one at (3,3) is not; from `{-1,-1}` the reverse.
  - from `{-1,1}`, a wall at (3,5) is in front of a figure at (4,4).
  - a wall level with the figure but 3 tiles to the side is not in front.
  - `compass` gives the four words above; `twistTurn(0.3)` is 0, `twistTurn(0.7)` is 1, `twistTurn(-0.7)` is -1.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `npx vitest run tests/view.test.js` — Expected: FAIL, module not found.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** `src/render/view.js` (no three.js import).
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** `npx vitest run tests/view.test.js` — Expected: PASS.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `viewOf`, `inFront` from Task 1.
- Produces:
  - `stage.turn(dir: 1|-1, { snap = false } = {}) -> Promise<void>` — `dir` 1 moves the camera clockwise round the room seen from above (south-east → south-west). Eases 300 ms (snap under `calm()`), ends exactly on the quarter (re-derived from the start angle, no drift).
